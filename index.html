About Rafayyl

A new standard for engineering & construction in Nigeria.

Founded in April 2024 and registered under RC No. 7451347, Rafayyl Engineering and Construction Limited brings together a multidisciplinary team of engineers, project managers, and procurement specialists committed to delivering projects that perform — on schedule, on budget, and to international standards.

From feasibility studies through commissioning, we manage every phase of your project. Our leadership team combines decades of experience in civil, structural, mechanical, and process engineering across Nigeria's most demanding sectors.

  • End-to-end delivery — from feasibility studies to commissioning and handover.
  • HSE-led culture aligned with global oil & gas and construction standards.
  • Local content depth combined with international engineering expertise.
  • ISO-aligned quality management systems on every project.
